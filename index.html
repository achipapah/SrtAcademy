Need to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay for a course?

Choose a course, sign in, and use the M-Pesa checkout. Approve the payment prompt on your phone. Access should be granted only after the payment callback is verified.

Can I learn on my Android phone?

Yes. This site is responsive and designed to work on phones. Some design software may require a computer, depending on the course.

When do I get access?

After the server verifies successful payment, your account is enrolled. Never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone or enter it on this website.

Are these courses accredited?

These are independent learning courses. Do not advertise a qualification or accreditation unless you have the authorization to provide it.
